# Blog Generator - Social Media Posts

## Target Audience (ICP)
Content Marketers, SEO Specialists, SaaS Founders, and Agency Owners.

**Pain Point Addressed:** Creating high-quality, SEO-optimized blog posts that actually rank takes hours of manual keyword research, outlining, writing, formatting, and finding media. Existing AI writing tools produce generic, thin content without proper structure or validated SEO data—they act as blind text generators.

**App Advantage:** A deterministic, multi-stage content assembly engine. Instead of a simple single-prompt generation, it analyzes top-ranking competitor URLs (via Playwright) to extract successful structures, pulls real-time Google Ads Keyword data for actual search volume, and assembles media-rich posts block-by-block using the Gemini API. The result is deep, highly-structured, E-E-A-T optimized content with automated YouTube/web citations in minutes, visible via a real-time streaming UI.
